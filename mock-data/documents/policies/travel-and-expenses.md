# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy

> Fictional training document. The amounts and procedures below are invented for demonstrations.

## 1. Booking business travel

Obtain manager approval before booking business travel. Use the company travel portal and select refundable options when the price difference is reasonable. Travel dates and business purpose must be included in the request.

## 2. Air and rail

Choose economy class for flights under six hours. For longer flights, employees may request a higher cabin only with written vice-president approval. Rail travel is preferred when the total door-to-door journey is under four hours and is practical for the business purpose.

## 3. Lodging and meals

Use preferred hotels when available. The mock standard lodging cap is **USD 220 per night before tax**. The mock meal allowance is **USD 65 per travel day**; receipts are required for individual meals above USD 25. These example limits are training data and are not actual Acme rates.

## 4. Expense submission

Submit expenses in the finance portal within **15 calendar days** after the trip ends. Include itemized receipts, business purpose, dates, and the applicable cost center. Personal expenses must not be included in a company claim.

## 5. International travel

International travel requires manager approval and a review of current destination guidance from the company travel desk. The travel desk can advise on entry requirements and business travel arrangements.

## 6. Questions not covered here

This document does not define whether personal devices may be used while abroad, provide destination-specific legal advice, or authorize access to restricted systems. Ask the travel desk or Security for those matters.
