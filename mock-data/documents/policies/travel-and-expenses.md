# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy

> Fictional training document. The amounts and procedures below are invented for demonstrations.

## 1. Booking business travel

Obtain manager approval before booking business travel. Use the company travel portal and select refundable options when the price difference is reasonable. Travel dates and business purpose must be included in the request.

## 2. Air and rail

Choose economy class for flights under six hours. For longer flights, employees may request a higher cabin only with written vice-president approval. Rail travel is preferred when the total door-to-door journey is under four hours and is practical for the business purpose.

## 3. Lodging and meals

Use preferred hotels when available. The mock standard lodging cap is **USD 220 per night before tax**. The mock meal allowance is **USD 65 per travel day**; receipts are required for individual meals above USD 25. These example limits are training data and are not actual Acme rates.

## 4. Expense submission

Submit expenses in the finance portal within **15 calendar days** after the trip ends. Include itemized receipts, business purpose, dates, and the applicable cost center. Personal expenses must not be included in a company claim.

## 5. International travel

International travel requires manager approval and a review of current destination guidance from the company travel desk. The travel desk can advise on entry requirements and business travel arrangements.

## 6. Expense categories (mock values)

| Category | Rule | Receipt needed |
|---|---|---|
| Taxi / rideshare | Allowed when public transport is impractical | Over USD 15 |
| Mileage | USD 0.60 per km for approved own-car use | Trip log |
| Parking | Reasonable, business related | Always |
| Client meals | USD 90 per person | Always, attendee names |
| Team events | Pre-approved by a director | Always |
| Alcohol | Not reimbursable | n/a |
| Mobile roaming | Business use only, within approved limit | Itemized bill |

## 7. Cost centers and approvals

| Claim total | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 500 | Line manager |
| USD 501 to 2,500 | Department head |
| Above USD 2,500 | Finance Operations director |

Claims must use the cost center of the project or team that benefits. Splitting a claim to avoid an approval threshold is not allowed.

## 8. Late and rejected claims

Claims after the 15-calendar-day window need written justification from the manager. Rejected claims state the reason; resubmit with corrections within 10 calendar days.

## 9. Worked examples

**Hotel.** A hotel at USD 240 per night before tax exceeds the USD 220 cap; the employee pays USD 20 per night unless the manager pre-approves the higher rate.

**Meals.** A single lunch of USD 30 needs a receipt; two meals at USD 20 each do not, but the day total must stay within USD 65.

**Trip ends Friday.** The claim is due 15 calendar days later, including weekends.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

- *Can I book a business-class flight?* Only for flights over six hours with written vice-president approval.
- *Are tips reimbursed?* Reasonable local tipping is reimbursed within the meal allowance.
- *What if I lose a receipt?* Submit a missing-receipt statement approved by your manager.
