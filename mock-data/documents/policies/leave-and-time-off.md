# Acme Corp Leave and Time-Off Policy

> Fictional training document. This policy is mock content for retrieval and citation exercises; it is not legal or employment advice.

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive an annual leave allowance stated in the HR system for their employee record. The allowance is prorated for eligible joiners and part-time schedules. The HR system is the source of truth for an individual's current balance; this document does not state any employee's personal balance.

## 2. Carry-forward limit

An employee may carry forward **up to five unused annual-leave days** into the next leave year. The carried-forward amount cannot exceed the employee's unused annual entitlement at year end. Unused carry-forward days expire on **31 March** of the new leave year and cannot be carried forward again. Local law or an individual employment agreement may provide different entitlements; People Operations must confirm exceptions.

## 3. Request notice and approval

Submit planned annual leave at least **five working days** before the first day away. Requests longer than five consecutive working days should be submitted at least **four weeks** in advance. A request is not approved until the employee's manager records an approval in the HR system. Do not make non-refundable arrangements before approval.

## 4. Sick leave

Notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible when illness prevents you from working. The HR system records sick leave separately from annual leave. A medical certificate may be requested for an absence longer than three consecutive working days, subject to applicable local requirements.

## 5. Holidays and working days

Weekends and company holidays are not counted as annual leave for employees whose schedule does not include those days. The employee's published work calendar determines the applicable holiday schedule.

## 6. Questions not covered here

This document does not determine an individual employee's balance, eligibility for a particular date, statutory rights, or exceptions in a local employment agreement. Contact People Operations for those questions.
