## Acme Corp Leave and Time-Off Policy

> Fictional training document. This policy is mock content for retrieval and citation exercises; it is not legal or employment advice.

### 1. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive an annual leave allowance stated in the HR system for their employee record. The allowance is prorated for eligible joiners and part-time schedules. The HR system is the source of truth for an individual's current balance; this document does not state any employee's personal balance.

### 2. Carry-forward limit

An employee may carry forward **up to five unused annual-leave days** into the next leave year. The carried-forward amount cannot exceed the employee's unused annual entitlement at year end. Unused carry-forward days expire on **31 March** of the new leave year and cannot be carried forward again. Local law or an individual employment agreement may provide different entitlements; People Operations must confirm exceptions.

### 3. Request notice and approval

Submit planned annual leave at least **five working days** before the first day away. Requests longer than five consecutive working days should be submitted at least **four weeks** in advance. A request is not approved until the employee's manager records an approval in the HR system. Do not make non-refundable arrangements before approval.

### 4. Sick leave

Notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible when illness prevents you from working. The HR system records sick leave separately from annual leave. A medical certificate may be requested for an absence longer than three consecutive working days, subject to applicable local requirements.

### 5. Holidays and working days

Weekends and company holidays are not counted as annual leave for employees whose schedule does not include those days. The employee's published work calendar determines the applicable holiday schedule.

### 6. Other leave types (mock values)

| Leave type | Entitlement | Notice | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parental leave | 16 weeks paid | 8 weeks where possible | Confirmation of date |
| Bereavement | 5 working days | As soon as possible | None |
| Jury or civic duty | As required by the summons | On receipt | Official notice |
| Study leave | Up to 3 days per year, manager approval | 4 weeks | Course enrolment |
| Unpaid leave | Up to 20 working days, People Operations approval | 6 weeks | Business case |

Statutory rights may exceed these values; People Operations confirms the applicable amount.

### 7. Worked examples

**Carry-forward.** An employee has 9 unused annual days on 31 December. Only 5 can be carried forward; the other 4 are lost. The 5 carried days must be used by 31 March.

**Notice.** A 3-day absence needs 5 working days notice. A 7-day absence needs 4 weeks notice.

**Public holiday inside a leave period.** A company holiday that falls on a working day within the leave period is not deducted as annual leave.

### 8. Cancelling or changing approved leave

Ask your manager to amend the request in the HR system. Cancelled days return to the balance. Changes within two working days of the start date need manager agreement and may not be possible.

### 9. Leave during notice period or probation

Leave taken in a probation period follows the same notice rules. Unused leave at the end of employment is settled according to the employment agreement and local law, not by this document.

### 10. Frequently asked questions

- *How much leave do I have left?* Check the HR system; this policy does not state any individual balance.
- *Can I carry forward more than five days?* No, unless People Operations confirms a local-law exception.
- *Does carried leave expire?* Yes, on 31 March, and it cannot be carried again.
- *Is my leave approved once I submit it?* No, only when your manager records approval.

## Acme Corp Hybrid Working and Office Guidelines

> Fictional training document.

### Summary

Employees may work remotely part of the week, subject to team agreements. This guide covers attendance, equipment, and office booking.

### 1. Attendance

| Role type | Expected office days |
|---|---|
| Office-based | 4 per week |
| Hybrid | 2 to 3 per week, agreed with the manager |
| Remote | As agreed in the employment terms |

### 2. Booking desks

Book desks in the workplace app up to 14 days ahead. Unused bookings release after 10:00.

### 3. Home working equipment

A one-time home-office allowance of USD 300 (mock) may be claimed through the finance portal with receipts. Company laptops must be used for company work and connect through the managed VPN.

### 4. Core hours

Teams agree core hours, typically 10:00 to 16:00 local time.

### 5. Working from another city or country

Working from another country for more than a short trip needs People Operations and tax review in advance. This guide does not cover device rules abroad.

### 6. Office facilities

Printers are on every floor. Report faults through the Service Desk. Visitors register at reception and use Acme-Guest.
